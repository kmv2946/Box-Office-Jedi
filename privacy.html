PRIVACY POLICY

Box Office Jedi is an independent fan project run by one person. It does not sell, rent or share your information, and it shows no ads that track you.

What we collect. If you create an account: your e-mail address, a display name you choose, and an optional location. If you play the Derby: your picks and any comment you write. We also store a one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself) to prevent duplicate entries, and brief sign-in records needed to keep you logged in.

What is public. Your display name, optional location and Derby score may appear on public leaderboards. Your e-mail address is never shown publicly.

How it is used. To run the Derby, send you sign-in links, and keep your entry history. Sign-in e-mails are sent through Resend. Site data is stored with Cloudflare. The newsletter is a separate list (Kit) that you join or leave independently.

Cookies. Signing in sets one cookie that keeps you logged in for 30 days. Signing out removes it.

Age. Accounts are for people 13 and older.

Deleting your account. E-mail the address on the About page from your account address and we will delete your account and entry history.
