FORECAST: ‘Mortal Kombat II’ Goes Up Against Meryl and Michael

by Box Office Jedi

Thursday, May 7, 2026

Here at Box Office Jedi, we like to go out on a limb.

Part of the satisfaction of box office analysis is getting things right. But a bigger thrill comes from how it stirs the imagination—when, before you put your official prediction down on paper, your mind takes off on what could happen, without constraints, with reality bending to your will. The Matrix Reloaded comes to mind. In my head, I saw it opening to $300 million domestic, or higher. I wouldn’t have bet my life on it, but these are movies we’re tracking after all, and what would the job be without a little fantasy?

Mortal Kombat II opens this weekend, challenging The Devil Wears Prada 2 for the top spot. Everyone seems confident MKII will pull it off, at least by a few million—while noting that it’s a closer race than expected. All it would take is a slightly softer opening from MKII and a better hold from Prada 2.

So what do we think here at Box Office Jedi? We’re going with Michael.

In its third weekend, Michael will reclaim the top spot from Prada 2 and fend off the new Mortal Kombat installment.

Yes, there’s a very likely scenario where the weekend looks like this:

  1. Mortal Kombat II — $48 million
  2. The Devil Wears Prada 2 — $40 million
  3. Michael — $30 million

But come out on this limb with us. It’s precarious, but the view is nice.

First, let’s talk about The Devil Wears Prada 2. Everyone’s hailing its opening weekend, calling it one of early summer’s biggest success stories, and in reality, yes, it did pretty well. But we all very quickly forgot that forecasts were at $95–105 million prior to last weekend, with some predicting higher. We went out on another kind of limb and guessed $108 million, sensing its kinship with Wicked and Barbie. Even after Friday numbers came out ($32.9 million), forecasters were predicting it’d end up in the 80s. Its opening weekend came in at $76.7 million.

Now, forecasters are suggesting a decent hold in its second frame, with solid reviews and Mother’s Day hitting on Sunday. A great hold here would be −44%, putting it around $43 million. That’s the ceiling. But we’re convinced that most people who are going to see Prada 2 saw it last weekend, and solid reviews and a lower-than-expected opening aren’t going to drive more buzz.

We collected a range of comps. The 2024 Mean Girls dropped −59% in its second weekend, but was coming off a holiday weekend, so a bigger drop was to be expected. Freakier Friday dropped −50% with no holiday weekend, but felt less saturated and frontloaded than Prada 2. Sex and the City, which feels most aligned with the Prada audience, dropped a whopping −62.8% in its second weekend. If Prada 2 were to take that kind of dive, it’d be at $28 million. A more likely scenario is Pitch Perfect 2’s second-weekend drop. It fell −55.5%, a decline that would put Prada 2 at $34.5 million.

Now, Mortal Kombat II. This could ride the video-game adaptation wave and easily open above $50 million this weekend. It could also crash and burn like the 2018 reboot of Tomb Raider ($23.6 million). We’re suspecting it will play out like another Lionsgate adaptation, Power Rangers, which opened to $40.3 million in 2017. Conveniently, when adjusted for inflation, Mortal Kombat: Annihilation’s 1997 opening comes out to $40.7 million. (Don’t forget you can toggle any chart on our site to see it adjusted for today’s ticket prices.)

But what if MKII just doesn’t click? Sure, it’s an easy #1 for Friday, and could open with around $16 million for the day. But it’s hugely frontloaded. It drops 30% on Saturday, falling to $11.2 million. Then, because no mothers are going to see MKII on Mother’s Day, it drops another 35%, putting it at $7.3 million for Sunday. That’s $34.5 million, exactly where we landed for Prada 2.

Michael has been consistently exceeding expectations. It soared past opening-weekend predictions, and then held extremely well in its second frame, considering its built-in fanbase and competition with Prada 2. If it falls another 44%, like last weekend, it lands softly at $30.5 million. But third-weekend drops are usually lighter, and we don’t sense that Michael Jackson fans are done with the experience. Even a −36% drop, which seems reasonable, would put it at $34.8 million—a hair above where we landed for MKII and Prada 2.

The major downside for Michael this weekend is that it’s losing its PLF screens to MKII and Billie Eilish. That will hurt it, but we sense a resilience with this movie. In a way, it feels more like a mom movie than Prada 2 does.

In other news, we’re predicting a breakout from The Sheep Detectives, the true Mother’s Day movie. If you talk to moms (or at least my mom), this is what they want.

In a quieter frame, Billie Eilish: Hit Me Hard and Soft — The Tour 3D feels like it could’ve been a major breakout, but the crowded slate this weekend is limiting its ceiling. It’ll still perform fine for a concert doc. The last documentary James Cameron directed, Aliens of the Deep, made just under $9 million. We’re guessing Billie opens there.

Our predictions for this weekend below. Wouldn’t this be fun?

1 Michael $37.4M
2 Mortal Kombat II $37.3M
3 The Devil Wears Prada 2 $37.2M
4 The Sheep Detectives $20.4M
5 Billie Eilish: Hit Me Hard and Soft — The Tour 3D $8.9M
6 The Super Mario Galaxy Movie $7.1M
7 Project Hail Mary $5.2M
8 Hokum $2.9M
9 Animal Farm $1.7M
10 Deep Water $1.2M
